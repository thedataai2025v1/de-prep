## How to study each scenario
1. Spend 10 minutes clarifying and drawing your own design.
2. Spend 20 minutes reviewing the architecture and trade-offs.
3. Spend 15 minutes answering principal-level follow-ups aloud.
4. Spend 10 minutes on failure modes, SLOs, and cost.
5. Redraw from memory and summarize in three minutes.

Architecture diagrams are conceptual, not deployment blueprints. Capacity figures are illustrative. Verify version-specific product behaviour against official documentation.
