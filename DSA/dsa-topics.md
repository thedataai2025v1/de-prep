## How to Use This Curriculum

### For Junior/Mid-Level DE Roles (0-3 years):
1. Master **Part 1** (Arrays & Hashing) — 80% of interview questions come from here
2. Add **Part 2** (Two Pointers) and **Part 3** (Sliding Window)
3. Practice each problem 2-3 times until you can solve them blindfolded

### For Senior DE Roles (3-5 years):
1. Complete **Parts 1-3** as foundation
2. Add **Part 4** (Binary Search) and **Part 5** (Linked List)
3. Be comfortable explaining trade-offs between approaches

### For Staff/Principal DE Roles (5+ years):
1. Master all **Parts 1-6**
2. Focus on optimization and system design integration
3. Be ready to discuss how these patterns apply to distributed data processing

### Interview Day Checklist:
- [ ] Can I solve 5 random array/hash problems in under 15 minutes each?
- [ ] Can I explain time/space complexity for every problem?
- [ ] Do I know at least 2 approaches for each problem?
- [ ] Can I identify the pattern (sliding window, two pointers, etc.) in a new problem?
