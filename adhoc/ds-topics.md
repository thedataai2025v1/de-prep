Detailed module-wise curriculum
Module 1 — AI & Machine Learning Fundamentals
Foundation
 8 lessons

Develop strong fundamentals in classical machine learning, algorithms, statistics and model selection.
1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Data Science
2. Supervised, Unsupervised and Reinforcement Learning
3. Regression Algorithms — Linear, Polynomial and Logistic Regression
4. Classification Algorithms — Decision Trees, Random Forest, SVM and Naive Bayes
5. Ensemble Learning — XGBoost, LightGBM, Gradient Boosting and CatBoost
6. Clustering, Recommendation Systems and Anomaly Detection
7. Feature Engineering, Feature Selection and Feature Stores
8. Model Evaluation, Statistical Metrics, Overfitting and Bias-Variance Trade-off
Principal-level focus: Selecting appropriate algorithms, understanding business objectives, model explainability, statistical validity and production suitability.
Module 2 — Deep Learning & Neural Networks
Foundation
 7 lessons

Understand the internal mechanics of neural networks and modern deep learning architectures.
1. Neural Network Fundamentals — Neurons, Layers and Activation Functions
2. Forward Propagation, Backpropagation and Gradient Descent
3. Loss Functions, Optimizers, Regularization and Normalization
4. CNN Architectures and Computer Vision
5. RNN, LSTM and GRU Architectures
6. Attention Mechanisms and Transformer Fundamentals
7. PyTorch and TensorFlow — Architecture, Training and Implementation
Principal-level focus: Model architecture selection, GPU training, optimization, distributed training and computational efficiency.
Module 3 — Generative AI & LLM Architecture
Core AI Engineering
 10 lessons

This is one of the most important modules for modern AI engineering interviews.
1. Introduction to Generative AI and Foundation Models
2. Transformer Architecture — Encoder, Decoder and Encoder-Decoder
3. Self-Attention, Multi-Head Attention and Positional Encoding
4. Tokenization, Embeddings and Context Windows
5. LLM Pretraining, Instruction Tuning and Alignment
6. LLM Fine-tuning — SFT, RLHF, DPO, LoRA and QLoRA
7. Quantization, Knowledge Distillation and Model Compression
8. Open-Weight versus Proprietary Models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama and Mistral
9. Multimodal AI — Vision, Audio, Video and Document Intelligence
10. Prompt Engineering, Context Engineering and Structured Generation
Principal-level focus: Understanding model internals, selecting models, adapting foundation models and balancing model quality, latency and infrastructure costs.
Module 4 — Data Engineering for AI & ML Pipelines
Important differentiator
 8 lessons

This module connects your existing Data Engineering expertise with modern AI engineering.
1. Designing End-to-End Data Pipelines for AI/ML
2. Training Data Collection, Ingestion and Dataset Preparation
3. Data Cleaning, Labeling, Validation and Quality Management
4. Feature Engineering and Feature Store Architecture
5. Batch versus Streaming Feature Engineering
6. Offline and Online Feature Stores — Point-in-Time Correctness
7. Data Versioning, Dataset Lineage and Reproducible ML
8. Training-Serving Skew, Data Leakage and Data Drift
Principal-level focus: Designing scalable AI-ready data platforms, reusable feature engineering infrastructure and reliable data-to-model pipelines.
Module 5 — Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) & Enterprise Knowledge Systems
Core AI Engineering
 10 lessons

RAG deserves its own module because enterprise AI interviews frequently test retrieval architecture, quality and production readiness.
1. RAG Fundamentals and End-to-End Architecture
2. Document Ingestion, Parsing and Preprocessing
3. Chunking Strategies — Fixed, Recursive, Semantic and Hierarchical
4. Embedding Models and Vector Representations
5. Vector Databases — FAISS, Milvus, Qdrant, Pinecone, Weaviate and pgvector
6. Retrieval Algorithms — Dense, Sparse, BM25 and Hybrid Search
7. Advanced Retrieval — Query Rewriting, HyDE, Multi-Query and Metadata Filtering
8. Reranking, Context Assembly and Grounded Generation
9. Advanced RAG — GraphRAG, Agentic RAG and Multi-Hop Retrieval
10. Enterprise RAG — Multi-Tenancy, Access Control, Evaluation and Optimization
Principal-level focus: Building enterprise-grade retrieval platforms that support millions of documents, multiple tenants, access controls, continuous updates and measurable answer quality.
Module 6 — AI Agents & Agentic Systems
Advanced AI Engineering
 8 lessons

Understand how to build reliable AI agents rather than just simple LLM-powered chat applications.
1. AI Agents — Fundamentals, Architecture and Execution Lifecycle
2. Reasoning, Planning and ReAct Architecture
3. Function Calling, Tool Use and External System Integration
4. Agent Memory, State Management and Context Management
5. LangGraph, LangChain and Agent Orchestration
6. Multi-Agent Systems, Delegation and Coordination
7. MCP, Human-in-the-Loop and Enterprise Tool Integration
8. Production AI Agents — Reliability, Evaluation, Security and Cost Optimization
Principal-level focus: Knowing when autonomous agents are appropriate, controlling execution, preventing unsafe actions and designing dependable multi-agent systems.
Module 7 — Model Training, Fine-tuning & Evaluation
Advanced ML Engineering
 8 lessons

This module builds the knowledge needed to discuss model development beyond API consumption.
1. End-to-End Model Training Lifecycle
2. Dataset Preparation, Training Splits and Data Leakage
3. Hyperparameter Optimization and Experiment Tracking
4. Distributed Model Training and GPU Utilization
5. Fine-tuning Foundation Models — SFT, LoRA and QLoRA
6. LLM Evaluation — Benchmarks, Golden Datasets and Human Evaluation
7. RAG and Agent Evaluation — Faithfulness, Relevance and Tool Accuracy
8. Model Validation, Regression Testing, A/B Testing and Model Selection
Principal-level focus: Determining whether a model is genuinely better, ensuring reproducibility and establishing standardized evaluation practices.
Module 8 — AI Inference, Model Serving & Infrastructure
Principal-level differentiator
 9 lessons

One of the most valuable modules for Staff and Principal AI Platform Engineering roles.
1. AI Model Deployment — Batch, Online and Real-Time Inference
2. LLM Serving Architecture and Model Lifecycle
3. GPU Architecture, Memory Management and GPU Scheduling
4. LLM Inference Internals — Prefill, Decode, KV Cache and Batching
5. Inference Optimization — Quantization, Speculative Decoding and Caching
6. Distributed Inference — Tensor Parallelism and Pipeline Parallelism
7. vLLM, NVIDIA Triton, TensorRT-LLM and Ray Serve
8. Model Routing, Load Balancing, Autoscaling and Rate Limiting
9. High-Performance AI Infrastructure — Capacity Planning, Latency and Cost Optimization
Principal-level focus: Designing inference platforms that support high throughput, low latency, efficient GPU utilization and predictable costs.
Module 9 — MLOps, LLMOps & AI Platform Engineering
Production engineering
 8 lessons

Learn how to operationalize AI at enterprise scale.
1. Introduction to MLOps and LLMOps Architecture
2. Experiment Tracking, Model Registry and Model Versioning
3. ML CI/CD and Automated Deployment Pipelines
4. Continuous Training and Automated Model Retraining
5. Model Monitoring, Drift Detection and Performance Monitoring
6. LLM Observability — Tracing, Tokens, Latency and Cost
7. AI Platform Architecture — Multi-Model and Multi-Team Infrastructure
8. Enterprise AI Platform — AWS SageMaker, Bedrock, Databricks, MLflow and Kubernetes
Principal-level focus: Creating shared AI capabilities, reusable infrastructure, standardized deployment patterns and operational excellence across teams.

Recommended study sequence
Although the modules are numbered 1–12, I would prioritize them strategically for your experience.
1. Phase 1 — Strengthen AI foundations
   
   Modules 1, 2 and 3. Build depth in ML, neural networks and LLM internals.
2. Phase 2 — Become strong in modern AI engineering
   
   Modules 5, 6 and 7. Master RAG, agents, fine-tuning and evaluation.
3. Phase 3 — Production and platform engineering
   
   Modules 4, 8 and 9. Connect AI to data pipelines, inference infrastructure and MLOps.
4. Phase 4 — Architecture and Principal readiness
   
   Modules 10, 11 and 12. System design, governance, strategy and leadership.
